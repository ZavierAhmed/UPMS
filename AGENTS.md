Applies to W02 and every future website milestone.

- Unique Plastic Industry is the only company represented by this website. Use only its official identity and approved graphite, orange and warm-white design language.
- Do not introduce Z.A Khan Packages or Unique Lubricants as affiliated companies, subsidiaries, sister companies, independent website brands, or alternate identities.
- Lubricant packaging remains a valid manufactured-product category. Describe packaging capabilities without implying that the company sells branded lubricant products.
- Review public copy, metadata, image content, alternative text, navigation and downloadable assets for identity confusion. Remove or replace conflicting public material using verified, approved assets only.
- Source brochures and unused local assets may contain other identities. Their presence is not approval to publish them. Preserve user-supplied source files; do not import or publish conflicting branding.
- Do not invent corporate relationships or modify the approved Unique Plastic design language.
