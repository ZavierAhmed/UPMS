New reconciliation and encrypted recovery understand both receipt forms and allocation arithmetic. Full financial policy is in EMPLOYEE-PAYMENT-CLASSIFICATION-POLICY.md.

No production privilege claim is inferred from these local tests.

Test waits were also corrected for hidden print headings and asynchronous source labels. These failures are not counted as passing acceptance.

Final results and artifact checks are captured in `evidence/final-validation.json`; individual JSON/logs remain the source of truth. Prior owner evidence modifications and unrelated source assets are preserved and excluded from this branch commit. No cheque, website, production settings, SMTP or backup-job changes are included.

Advances, partial allocation, reclassification reversal and refund after allocation remain blocked. No historical owner transaction has been automatically classified, renumbered or reposted. This is an isolated UAT branch, not production activation readiness.
