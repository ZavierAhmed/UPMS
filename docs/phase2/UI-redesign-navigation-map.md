# Management navigation map

All entries are permission-gated, with visible active-page state. No unsupported global reports/policy pages are fabricated.

| Group | Entry | Existing destination |
|---|---|---|
| Workspace | Overview | Role-aware dashboard and genuine workspace links |
| Operations | Inventory | Stock, documents, history, existing configuration/reconciliation |
| Operations | Purchases and accounts | Receipts/invoices, vendor accounts, money journals and reconciliation |
| Operations | Sales / Delivery Challans | Challans, Customer ledger, configuration/reconciliation |
| Operations | Customer Returns | Requests, physical quarantine receipt, inspection/disposition |
| Accounts | Customer Receipts | Settled receipts and existing-credit applications |
| Accounts | Cheque Management | Pending custody, deposit, clearance, exact-approved dishonour |
| Accounts | Customer credit notes | Price/return credit review and financial posting |
| Accounts | Business expenses | Categories, obligations, later settlement and reconciliation |
| Administration | Master data | Existing master, settings, approvals and audit controls |
| Administration | Staff accounts | Existing users/department permissions and sessions |
| Administration | Identity history | Existing audited identity events |
| Administration | My profile | Password/session controls |

Accounts-only entries do not appear for Inventory. Sales sees permitted challans, credits and physical returns, not expenses/receipts. Sidebar groups omit empty entries; backend permissions remain authoritative. A password-change-required account remains restricted to its profile. Sign-out clears authenticated UI and private read cache.

Dashboard quick cards open a permitted workspace; choose its named primary action to begin a document. They do not silently discard or replace a retained draft. Vendor accounts remain in Purchases; customer GST/NON_GST ledgers remain separately selectable in Sales. Policies, approvals and reconciliation remain under their actual existing module controls.
