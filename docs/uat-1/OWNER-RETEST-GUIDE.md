# Owner retest guide — local UAT only

Use [the existing local dashboard](http://127.0.0.1:5173/management) and refresh it after the update. Keep the same assigned local credentials. The preview retains its fixed `tmp/php-runtime/owner-uat-binding.json` database binding; moving automated-fixture pointers cannot replace your database on restart. Your records, administrators and settings were not reset or migrated.

1. **Popups:** start an expense and enter description, amount, date/reference and ordinary payee. Open Add Category and Add Employee. Cancel, Escape and save an inline record. Check that labels stay horizontal at narrow and wide sizes, Tab/Shift-Tab stay in the dialog, focus returns to the opener and unrelated expense values remain.
2. **Employee payee:** select an employee-based category and an active employee. Separate editable Payee disappears; Intended payee shows the employee. Confirm the business-expense acknowledgement. Save/review the draft. Changing/deactivating the employee or category requires review/resave before posting. Ordinary categories retain required editable Payee. Existing posted expenses keep their original payee/snapshots; they are not silently reclassified. This is not salary, an advance or a loan.
3. **Browser navigation:** client ledger → Money Accounts → Internal transfer. Browser Back must show account cards; Forward must show transfer. Enter an unsaved amount, then Back/Forward; it must remain. Do not post a real transfer for this navigation check.
4. **Items:** inspect stock-in and challan forms. Expect Items/Add item/Remove item and Item details. Quantities still count bottles. Selectors, pricing and available-stock checks retain existing behavior.
5. **Custom challan number:** enter a unique local test value such as `UPI-UAT-001`. Save/edit a draft, then finalize only if you intend a local UAT stock/receivable transaction. Official DC numbering stays separate and unchanged. A second finalized challan with the same custom number must fail, including case/outer-space variants. Drafts may share a candidate until one posts. Search by either number and check both on print. Supplier references remain separate.
6. **Weekly reports:** client Hafta remains a client sales/payment statement. Follow Weekly business expenses or use the week preset on Expense Reports; choose category/employee/account as needed. A category called Hafta is not client activity. Signed full expense refunds stay negative on their own date.
7. **Print/Excel:** check Company Transactions, client/vendor ledgers, money statements, expense groups, client Hafta, challans, purchases, stock/movements and financial documents. Print Preview should show the approved logo/title/context, readable columns, essential opening/closing amounts and references; controls and unrelated dashboard cards should be absent. Compare spreadsheet rows and totals.

Custom number policy: outer whitespace is trimmed, ASCII letters are uppercased, maximum 40 characters, single hyphen/slash separators, no internal whitespace or Unicode lookalikes. DC/CR/VP/RF prefixes followed by a separator are reserved to avoid confusing internal references. Literal O/0 and I/1 remain different characters; verify your approved number carefully. Legacy external references are not backfilled as custom numbers.

The local UAT environment is not production. Do not use production credentials, genuine stock/payments or hosted release steps. Existing cheque/payroll/GST limitations and production blockers remain. Screenshots and sample PDFs under this milestone are explicitly synthetic, not your records.
